# Free vs Pro

Ektyra has a free core and a paid **Pro** tier. Pro items are marked **(Pro)** in their normal group, for example
_Data Display / Kanban board (Pro)_.

## How a license works

Pro lives in a separate private package. A license is **access to that package** (a registry token): no token, no
install. Nothing checks the license while your dashboard runs, so it can never break production.

The full reasoning and the open questions (pricing, updates, white label) are in `docs/free-vs-pro.md`.
